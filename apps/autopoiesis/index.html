How this organism works

A grid of cells lives under a Life-like rule: each generation, a cell is born, survives, or dies by counting its living neighbors. Every birth is caused by an already-living neighbor and fires a sound — so the piece never loops on a clock, it cascades outward the way one falling domino tips the next. Column sets stereo position, row sets depth (filtering, reverb send, loudness). Every few generations the system drifts through four structural phases — Genesis, Cascade, Density, Decay — that slowly reshape the tempo of the automaton, the reverb, and the delay, so the whole piece's form keeps changing. Loaded samples are played back as short, windowed grains at random positions (sometimes reversed) rather than straight one-shots, in the spirit of tape-based musique concrète. Click or drag on the colony to seed new life anywhere in the field.
